# Scope

## In scope

- Nationwide federal district civil duration and pending-inventory analytics from 2010.
- Typed intake and milestone forecast refusals with safe observed-data alternatives.
- RECAP operational metadata only where matching and field-quality gates pass.
- Preserved censoring-aware baseline and challenger failure evidence.
- Synthetic resource and budget sensitivity scenarios.
- Dual operations and matter-planning workflows.
- Seeded deidentified offline demo.

## Out of scope

- Legal advice or case-outcome prediction.
- Judge, party, attorney, or document-text profiling.
- Real legal-cost forecasts or billing benchmarks.
- PACER purchases or private client data.
- Unreviewed source matching.
- Live warehouse access from public container.
- Matter-specific duration predictions or forecast-readiness claims.

## Release gates

See [metric glossary](metric-glossary.md). Failed model gates require typed forecast refusal. Observed benchmarks never become matter-specific estimates. Deployment and publication remain separate approval gates.
