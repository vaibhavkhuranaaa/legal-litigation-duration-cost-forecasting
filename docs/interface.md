# Interface contract

The product is a full-population analytics workspace backed by the same versioned artifact in the static Pages build and offline API.

## Portfolio intelligence

One persistent analytical scope controls the executive measures, filing trend, district and case-family rankings, pending-age distribution, cohort context, evidence status, and exported view. Users can filter 94 districts and 14 nature families, inspect 17 annual filing cohorts, compare four pending-age bands, and move directly from a ranking into the selected portfolio slice.

Exact national and marginal totals use every governed record. District-by-family cells below 200 records remain explicitly withheld. Observed duration remains a terminated-case description, not a forecast. The procedural-cohort comparison is an observed administrative-termination benchmark through the disclosed development cutoff.

## Scenario lab

The scenario lab remains a connected secondary workspace. Its low, base, and high staffing and budget outputs are deterministic sensitivity calculations from user-entered assumptions. They use no observed cost data and are not duration or cost forecasts.

## Shared boundaries

- Capability readiness remains visible without dominating the analytics workspace.
- Observed, synthetic, unavailable, and refusal states have explicit text and color treatment.
- Data export includes the active filters, displayed aggregate slice, definitions, publication policy, and provenance.
- Loading, connection error, retry, empty, and result states are present.
- Keyboard focus, labels, landmarks, reduced-motion handling, and responsive layouts are part of the release contract.

The product is an analytics and sensitivity instrument, not legal advice.
