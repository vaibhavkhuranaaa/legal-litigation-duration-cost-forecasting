# Interface contract

The local interface has two task-focused workspaces backed by the versioned API.

## Portfolio

The portfolio view reports governed aggregate workload, pending inventory, case-identity coverage, and reviewed RECAP match availability. The full-population explorer filters 94 districts and 14 nature families, shows 17 annual filing cohorts, and breaks pending inventory into four age bands. Exact national and marginal totals use every governed record; district-by-family cells below 200 records are explicitly withheld. Observed duration remains a terminated-case description, not a forecast.

The procedural-cohort chart remains a separate observed administrative-termination benchmark through the disclosed development cutoff. It is not a matter-specific prediction.

## Matter planner

The planner refuses predicted completion dates and directs users to observed cohort context. Its low, base, and high staffing and budget outputs are deterministic sensitivity calculations from user-entered assumptions. They use no observed cost data and are not real cost forecasts.

## Shared boundaries

- Capability readiness remains visible in both workspaces.
- Observed, synthetic, unavailable, and refusal states have explicit text and color treatment.
- Data export includes the active filters, displayed aggregate slice, definitions, publication policy, and provenance.
- Loading, connection error, retry, empty, and result states are present.
- Keyboard focus, labels, landmarks, reduced-motion handling, and responsive layouts are part of the release contract.

The product is an operations planning instrument, not legal advice.
