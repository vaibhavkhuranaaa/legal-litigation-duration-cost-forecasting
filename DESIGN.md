# User-facing design rules

- Use restrained, audit-friendly operational design.
- Use accessible type, contrast, focus, keyboard behavior, and reduced motion.
- Show loading, empty, sparse-data, stale-data, error, and refusal states.
- Put definition, method, source snapshot, sample size, and limitation beside every number.
- Distinguish observed public metadata, model estimates, and synthetic scenarios in words and visual treatment.
- Never imply legal advice, outcome certainty, or real cost forecasting.
